How a wash goes

From muddy to marvellous

Every customer brings a filthy ride and a funny request. Bubbles hands you the right tool and you make it shine.

1

Pick your next customer

Follow the road through 300 levels, from Sunny Beach to Muddy Farm and on to Speed City. Each stop brings a new animal customer and a new mess.

2

Blast, foam and scrub

Spray away thick mud, pile on fluffy purple foam and scrub until the paint shines through. Each tool has its own feel, sound and buzz.

3

Sweat the small stuff

Trace tyre treads, polish rims and headlights, swipe the mirrors, then head inside to vacuum crumbs, wipe the dash and hang the duck air freshener.

4

Make it yours

Earn stars, coins and tips. Unlock vehicles for relaxing Free Wash, then style them with paint, wraps, rims and underglow.
