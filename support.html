FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my purchases back on a new iPhone?

Open the Store from the map and tap Restore Purchases (on the Premium, Offers or Style tab). Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

Premium vehicles, style packs, bundles, VIP Garage, Remove Ads and the Starter Pack all come back. Coin packs are used up when you spend the coins, so they can't be restored.

I paid but didn't get my item.

Close the app completely and open it again: it checks with Apple at launch and unlocks anything you own. If it's still missing, tap Restore Purchases. If that doesn't work either, email us with the item name and purchase date from your Apple receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so refunds come from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

How do I turn off ads?

Reward ads (like “x2 coins”) only play when you tap to watch one. Ads between levels start at level 10 and never appear while you're washing. To remove them, buy Remove Ads or the Starter Pack on the Store's Offers tab.

Can I stop purchases on my child's iPhone?

Yes. Go to Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases → Don't Allow. You can also turn on Ask to Buy through Family Sharing.

I reset my progress by mistake. Can you restore it?

Sorry, no. Your progress is saved only on your iPhone and we never receive a copy. Anything you bought with real money isn't lost: tap Restore Purchases.

Does my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on the device. If your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) includes the app's data, restoring that backup brings your progress with it. Purchases always come back with Restore Purchases.

A spot won't come clean. What do I do?

Keep going: missed spots glow, and Splashy points them out if you pause for a moment. If a station really won't finish, email us the level number and vehicle and we'll fix it.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm.

Close other apps and check that Low Power Mode is off. The game adjusts its graphics on its own. If it stays slow, email us your iPhone model.

Do I need the internet to play?

No. Washing works offline. The internet is only used for ads and purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game saves is on your iPhone. Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or delete the app. There's no account and nothing stored on our side. For the ad data Google collects, see “Your rights” in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy: Splashy's Car Wash

Last updated: [PUBLISH DATE]

Splashy's Car Wash (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a game for iPhone made by [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME]. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your iPhone. We don't run servers, so we can't see it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. They are always non-personalised. The app never asks to track you and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Apple handles purchases. We never see your payment details.

1. Information kept on your device

To remember your game, the app saves files only on your iPhone:

  • your progress: levels finished, stars, coins, upgrades, daily rewards and collection album;
  • the vehicles, foam colours and styles you unlocked, and how you styled each vehicle;
  • the text you typed on your neon sign (up to 7 letters);
  • your settings (music, sounds, voices, vibration);
  • a record of purchases the app has already given you, so nothing is given twice. It's protected with a key made from your device's identifier for this app; the identifier stays on your device and is never sent to us;
  • a short log of game events (such as “level 12 finished”), at most 500 of them, used for things like daily rewards. This log is never sent anywhere.

You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by deleting the app. Real-money purchases stay yours: tap Restore Purchases in the Store to get them back on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Reward ads are always your choice: you tap a button (for example “x2 coins”) to watch one.
  • Ads between levels may appear after you finish a level, from level 10 onwards, and never while you're washing. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns them off.

Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads, and the app never shows Apple's “Allow tracking?” prompt. Even so, to deliver ads, limit repeats, prevent fraud and measure results, Google's software in the app automatically collects some information, such as:

  • your device's IP address, which can show your approximate location (roughly your city or region);
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language) and an identifier for this app on your device;
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps);
  • crash and performance information from the ad software.

We don't link this information to you, and we don't receive it in a form that identifies you. Google handles it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. Where the law asks for your consent to ads (for example the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message when it first starts. It also shows Google's message in US states that give similar rights, where it applies. [If you add a “Privacy choices” button in Settings, say: You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices.]

3. In-app purchases

You can buy optional items: premium vehicles, style packs, bundles, VIP Garage, Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and coin packs. Apple processes every payment. We never see or store your card details, name or Apple Account; Apple only tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. What the app does not do

  • It doesn't create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • It doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • It doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, or ask permission to.
  • It doesn't sell your personal information.
  • It has no chat, messaging or way to share content with other players.
  • It doesn't send your game progress or event log to us or anyone else.

5. Children

Splashy's Car Wash is made for a general audience and is not in the App Store's Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts, chat or sharing; ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (Google's “G” rating) and are never personalised. If you're a parent and think your child has given us information, email us and we'll help.

6. Your rights

We don't hold personal information about you on any server, so usually there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete: your progress lives on your device, and you can erase it with Reset progress or by deleting the app.

For information Google collects for ads, use the consent message in the app (where it's shown), reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in iPhone Settings, or contact Google using the links above. If you live in the EEA, the UK, California or another place with privacy laws, you can contact us with any request and we'll answer within the time the law requires.

7. Security

Your progress stays on your device and is protected by iOS. Payments go through Apple. We don't run our own servers that could be breached.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
